# Clickroom Trademark Policy

The source code of Clickroom is open source (AGPL-3.0, with MIT SDKs). The
**name "Clickroom", the Clickroom logo, and related branding are not** covered
by those licenses. This policy explains what you may and may not do with them.

_Owner of the marks: [TO BE CONFIRMED BY THE PROJECT OWNER]._

## You may, without asking

- Self-host Clickroom for yourself or your organization and refer to it as
  Clickroom.
- Make accurate, factual references: "works with Clickroom", "a Clickroom
  integration", "migrating from X to Clickroom", in articles, talks, docs and
  comparisons.
- Use the unmodified logo to link to or identify the official project, e.g. on
  a "powered by" or integrations page, without implying endorsement.
- Publish SDK wrappers, plugins and tools whose names make clear they are
  unofficial (for example "acme-clickroom-exporter", not "Clickroom Exporter").

## You need written permission to

- Offer a hosted or managed service (a "cloud", "SaaS" or resold product) under
  the Clickroom name or a confusingly similar name.
- Use the name or logo in a company, product, domain or social-account name.
- Use the marks in a way that suggests official status, partnership or
  endorsement.

## Forks and modified versions

You are free to fork under the license terms. A fork or substantially modified
distribution **must be renamed** and must remove the Clickroom logo and
branding, unless the project owner has agreed otherwise in writing. You may
state that your project "is based on Clickroom".

## Questions and permission requests

Open an issue or contact the maintainers using the contact in SECURITY.md.
