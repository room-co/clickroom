# Dashboards

The **Dashboard** page (the first item in the sidebar, and the page you land on) has two parts.

## Overview

The top of the page shows live numbers for the selected project: **Total Events**, **Event Types**,
**Active Users** and **Visitors**, an **Events over time** chart, **Top Pages** (with sessions,
clicks and click rate), and the **Last 10 events**.

Use the date range control in the page header to pick the period (presets such as last 7, 14 or 30
days, or a custom range) and the refresh control to auto-refresh every 5 seconds, 10 seconds, 30
seconds or 1 minute, or turn auto-refresh off.

## Widgets

Below the overview is a board of **widgets**. A widget is a saved [insight](product-analytics.md)
pinned to the dashboard.

To add one:

- On the dashboard, click **Add widget** and choose a saved insight, or
- From **Product analytics**, open an insight and choose **Add to dashboard**.

On the board you can drag widgets to rearrange and resize them. Each widget has a menu with **Edit**
(opens the insight) and **Remove**. Removing a widget does not delete the insight. A widget can use
its own date range instead of the one stored in the insight.

Anyone with the Member or Admin role can change the board. Viewers can see it.
