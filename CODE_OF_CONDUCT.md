# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
by reference.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Encouraged: empathy and kindness, respect for differing opinions, giving and gracefully accepting
constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable: sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal
or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information without
permission, and other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or
reject contributions, and ban contributors, for behaviour they deem inappropriate. This applies in
all project spaces and when representing the project in public.

Report abuse privately to the maintainers at **conduct@<domain>** (placeholder until the project
domain is final). All reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is
respected. Maintainers follow the
[Enforcement Guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
(correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
